# FoodSafe365 — Simplified HACCP Controls UX

This build simplifies the HACCP area for restaurant supervisors.

## UX decision
The HACCP area is now one page: **HACCP Controls**.

It shows:
- the 7 HACCP principles in simple language;
- an FSSAI-aligned operational checklist;
- key process controls for cooking, cooling cooked food, hot holding and reheating;
- FSSAI reference guidance and the outlet's validated process when available;
- the operational flow: Understand → Check → Correct → Verify → Record.

Separate "Critical controls" and "Monitoring" pages are removed from the supervisor-facing navigation. Monitoring continues through Daily Checks; deviations continue through Actions Centre; history remains in Records.

## Important regulatory/product distinction
FSSAI reference guidance is not automatically a CCP or a universal critical limit. The outlet's approved/validated HACCP process remains the control reference. FoodSafe365 is FSSAI-aligned, not FSSAI-certified, and does not represent its self-monitoring as the official FSSAI Hygiene Rating.
