# Sprint 12 — Priority Temperature Controls

## HACCP Controls UX
The HACCP Controls page now presents exactly five priority temperature-control cards in this order:

1. Storage
2. Cooking
3. Cooling
4. Thawing
5. Reheating

The supervisor-facing page remains a single simple page. Critical Controls and Monitoring are not exposed as separate navigation pages.

## FSSAI reference guidance shown in the cards
- Storage: animal-origin foods ≤4°C; cold foods ≤5°C; frozen foods ≤−18°C; also highlights segregation and FIFO/FEFO.
- Cooking: vegetarian 60°C/10 min or 65°C/2 min core; non-vegetarian 65°C/10 min, 70°C/2 min or 75°C/15 sec core.
- Cooling: 60°C → 21°C within ≤2 h, then 21°C → 5°C within a further ≤2 h.
- Thawing: meat/fish/poultry ≤5°C in refrigerator or microwave; shellfish/seafood ≤15°C in cold potable running water within 90 min; no thawed food stored for later use.
- Reheating: 75°C core for at least 2 min.

These are displayed as FSSAI reference guidance, not as universal HACCP critical limits. Outlet-specific validated processes remain authoritative.

Source: FSSAI Hygiene Rating material: https://hygiene.fssai.gov.in/
