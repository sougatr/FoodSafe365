## 2. Google Play Full Description (ASO-Optimized)

> **Copy everything below into the "Full description" box in Google Play Console (up to 4,000 characters):**

```text
Know your food is safe before you take the first bite.

FoodSafe365 empowers diners, families, and food businesses to make food safety visible, verifiable, and transparent. Inspired by recent FDA and FSSAI hygiene crackdowns across Maharashtra, FoodSafe365 brings back-of-house kitchen standards into the public light.

🔍 FOR DINERS & CONSUMERS:
• Rate any Kitchen for Food Safety: Complete a 20-second, 5-pillar food safety observation directly from your table.
• Scan Table QR Codes: Instant table-level attribution with no heavy app downloads or endless registration forms.
• See Daily Kitchen Records: Check verified morning logs before ordering—including walk-in chiller temperatures (≤5°C), cooking oil Total Polar Materials (TPM ≤25%), and purified water filter servicing.
• Discreet Direct Alerts: Notice an issue like staff not wearing gloves? Alert the restaurant manager privately to resolve it before you leave, without awkward public confrontations.
• Mumbai Pilot Directory: Explore verified restaurants, cafes, and eateries across Colaba, Bandra, Lower Parel, and beyond.

🏪 FOR RESTAURANTS, CAFES & CLOUD KITCHENS:
• 2-Minute Morning Checklist: Log daily fridge temperatures and oil quality to earn the "Verified Kitchen" seal.
• Stand Out from Competitors: Turn food safety and FSSAI compliance into your biggest marketing advantage. Clean kitchens don’t fear inspections!
• Printable Table QR Kit: Download ready-to-print Table Tent Cards and bill folder stickers with 1 tap.
• Resolve Diner Feedback: Receive real-time alerts when guests notice hygiene gaps, enabling immediate resolution and loyal repeat customers.

🛒 FOR GROCERY STORES & SUPERMARKETS:
• Cold storage breach alerts (dairy, fresh produce, meat).
• Receiving checks and expiry rotation (FIFO/FEFO).

WHY FOOD SAFETY MATTERS:
Repeatedly heated cooking oil produces harmful polar compounds, broken cold chains cause rapid bacterial growth, and unfiltered water leads to severe infections. FoodSafe365 gives you the proof before you order.

Food safety is a priority, not an afterthought. Download FoodSafe365 today!
```
